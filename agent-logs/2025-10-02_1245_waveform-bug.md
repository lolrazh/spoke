## Key Learnings
- **Tailwind utilities can override custom keyframes** - When using Tailwind’s `animate-*` utilities, their keyframes may replace similarly named global animations.
- **Namespacing animations prevents collisions** - Unique keyframe names maintain isolation across feature areas and third-party utility classes.

## Architecture Decisions
- **Scoped pill animation** - Opted to rename the keyframe instead of disabling Tailwind utilities to keep future onboarding animations flexible.
- **Trade-off:** Slightly less semantic animation name in exchange for complete isolation and zero impact on Tailwind usage elsewhere.
